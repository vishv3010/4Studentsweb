# 🎬 4Students • Video Editor Onboarding Form Generator

This Google Apps Script automatically builds the official **Video Editor Onboarding Form** directly inside your Google Drive for **4studentshub@gmail.com**, complete with:
- Personal & Contact details
- Residential Address
- Passport-size Photograph placeholder
- College & Enrollment Verification (Degree, Branch, Year, College ID Card)
- Video Editing Software
- **Official Terms & Conditions** (₹150 per reel, 15–20 reels/month or fewer, turnaround time, confidentiality)
- **UPI ID for Payouts**
- **Explicit Agreement Checkbox**
- **Auto-created Google Sheet** linked directly to record all responses in real-time

*(Removed: Portfolio links, workstation specs, and emergency contact per your instruction).*

---

## ⚡ Quick Update & Run Instructions

1. Go back to your Google Apps Script editor tab at **[https://script.google.com](https://script.google.com)**.
2. Replace all the code in `Code.gs` with the updated script from [`create_video_editor_form.gs`](./create_video_editor_form.gs).
3. Press **`Ctrl + S`** to save.
4. Click **`▶ Run`**.
5. Once it finishes running, check the **Execution log** at the bottom for your updated Form Edit URL, Public Share Link, and Linked Sheet!
